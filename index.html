Questions

Good to know

Do my free scans reset if I reinstall?

No. Scans are counted on your account, so reinstalling or switching phones doesn't reset them, and you don't lose any.

I changed phones. Will my contacts still be there?

Yes. Your contacts, cards and notes are saved to your account, not your phone. Install Scaname from Google Play and sign in the same way you did before (email, Google or Apple), and everything will be there.

Can I get a refund?

Within 48 hours of buying, you can request a refund through Google Play, and it's usually automatic. After that, refunds go through Google Play's standard process. Our Terms of Service have the details.

How do I get my contacts out of Scaname?

Go to Settings, then Your data, then Export all contacts (CSV). It's free, with no unlock needed. You can also share any single contact as a contact file.

Can it read digital business cards?

Yes. Point the camera at a contact QR code (another Scaname user's card, or most digital-card apps) and the contact fills in instantly. QR codes don't use a scan, even on the free plan.

Does Scaname work offline?

Mostly. You can browse your contacts and your card without a connection, and changes sync when you're back online. Scanning needs a connection, because the AI reads the card.
